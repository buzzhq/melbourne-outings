Hikes

  • Sherbrooke Falls walk, Dandenong Ranges NP
    Any day (daytime)Sherbrooke or O'Donohue Picnic Ground, near KallistaFree

    Short rainforest walk through mountain ash and tree ferns to a small falls, best after spring rain; night deer-control closures run in parts of the park until 19 Nov, so keep it a daytime outing.

  • Sugarloaf Peak via Wells Cave Track, Cathedral Range State Park
    Any day (not on Total Fire Ban days)Sugarloaf Saddle, Cathedral Range SPFree

    A scramble through Wells Caves to the 920 m top of the range; Parks Vic says spring and early summer bring native orchids and are ideal for longer hikes here. Hard, not for young kids.

  • Flinders Peak Lookout, You Yangs Regional Park
    Daily 7am to 8pmTurntable car park, Little RiverFree

    450 steps and 200 m of climb to views over Melbourne and Geelong, an hour from the city; go early before the summer heat and crowds.

Bike trails

  • Ballarat-Skipton Rail Trail
    Any dayBallarat to Skipton (parking at Smythesdale and Linton)Free

    54 km of mostly concrete and fine gravel through Smythesdale and Linton, with the old Nimons Bridge as the highlight; easy to do in sections.

  • Main Yarra Trail, Southbank to Westerfolds Park
    Any daySouthbank to TemplestoweFree

    About 33 km out along the river through Yarra Bend, mostly flat and sealed with a few short steep pinches; a classic long city ride straight from the CBD.

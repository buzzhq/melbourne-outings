Hikes

  • Hanging Rock Summit Walk, Hanging Rock Reserve
    Any day139 South Rock Rd, Woodend (under an hour from Melbourne)$15 per car

    A moderate 1.8 km return climb (60–90 min) through boulders to views of Mt Macedon and Camels Hump; go early or late on warm days, when grey kangaroos graze nearby.

  • Organ Pipes walk and picnic, Organ Pipes National Park
    Any day (mornings in summer)Organ Pipes NP, near Keilor NorthFree

    Basalt columns, Rosette Rock and the Tessellated Pavement about 1.5 hrs on foot, a short drive from the city; Parks Vic suggests morning to mid-afternoon in summer, and spring can bring kangaroo and wallaby joeys. Steep path.

  • Steavenson Falls Walk, Marysville
    Any day; falls floodlit dusk to midnightFalls Rd, Marysville (about 2 hrs from Melbourne)$3 parking

    Short all-abilities walk to the base of one of Victoria's tallest waterfalls (84 m), with stone steps up to a top lookout; a warm-evening visit to see the falls floodlit is the twist.

  • Cape Schanck Lighthouse Reserve boardwalk
    Daily 6am–6pmCape Schanck, Mornington PeninsulaFree

    Short circuit, then the staircase and boardwalk down to the rock platform with lookouts over Pulpit Rock and Devils Desk; good for sunsets and coastal wildflowers. Watch for big waves on the rocks.

Bike trails

  • You Yangs mountain bike trails (Kurrajong Plantation & Stockyards)
    Any dayYou Yangs Regional Park, Little RiverFree

    Over 50 km of signed trails: Kurrajong's 20 km of flatter loops suit families, while Stockyards has rocky singletrack and three downhill runs.

  • Federation Trail, Brooklyn to Werribee
    Any dayBrooklyn to Werribee (west)Free

    A 23 km off-road path along the old outfall sewer, flat and easy, with the Brooklyn section freshly resurfaced; pair it with lunch in Werribee.

  • Great Southern Rail Trail
    Any dayNyora to Yarram, South Gippsland (about 130 km SE of Melbourne)Free

    136 km of crushed granite and fine gravel through rolling South Gippsland dairy country; ride a section as a day trip or do it over a weekend.

  • Murray to Mountains Rail Trail
    Any dayWangaratta to Bright, plus Yackandandah and Rutherglen branchesFree

    137 km of sealed trail into the High Country; an early-summer weekend away with wineries and Bright at the end.

Hikes

  • Bushrangers Bay Walk, Mornington Peninsula National Park
    Any day (spring coastal wildflowers)Cape Schanck (eastern carpark)Free

    5.4 km return (about 2 hrs) through coastal scrub to a wild basalt bay; spring brings out the coastal windflowers. No swimming here.

  • Camels Hump, Macedon Regional Park
    Any dayMount MacedonFree

    The park's 1,011 m high point, with views across to Hanging Rock; pair it with the Memorial Cross walk and spring gardens on the mountain.

  • Beeripmo Walk, Mount Buangor National Park
    Any day (usually 2 days)Mount Buangor NP, near BeaufortFree

    21 km Grade 3 overnighter with a campground halfway, in a park newly proclaimed a national park on 5 Oct; some former state-park areas are still closed after the 2024 fire.

Bike trails

  • 10,000 Steps Loop Trail, Woowookarung Regional Park
    Any dayWoowookarung Regional Park, BallaratFree

    New 8 km multi-use loop opened for the park's 10th birthday, with a lookout; spring wildflowers line the route.

  • Bellarine Rail Trail
    Any daySouth Geelong to QueenscliffFree

    35 km mostly flat ride from South Geelong (train-friendly) through farmland to Queenscliff; finish with fish and chips by the water.

  • O'Keefe Rail Trail
    Any dayBendigo to HeathcoteFree

    49 km rail trail from Bendigo station; Axedale makes a 23 km turnaround, or ride on to Heathcote's wineries.

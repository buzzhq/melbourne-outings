Hikes

  • Rainforest Gallery and Mt Donna Buang, Yarra Ranges NP
    Any day (a cool-weather escape on hot days)Acheron Way / Donna Buang Rd, above WarburtonFree

    A 40 m elevated walkway 15 m above cool temperate rainforest, with Mountain Ash and Myrtle Beech up to 400 years old, plus a 20-minute stair walk along Cement Creek; then the 1.2 km-high summit lookout. Check the fire danger rating before heading to the ranges.

  • Warburton Redwood Forest and Cement Creek loop
    Any day; go mid-week or early to beat crowdsCement Creek Rd, East WarburtonFree

    Over 1,470 Californian redwoods, planted in the 1930s and now 55 m tall, in shady rows; loop down to the Yarra through ferns, or do the 16 km return O'Shannassy Aqueduct Trail from Yuonga Rd. Shade makes it a good warm-day pick.

  • Two Bays Walking Trail, Mornington Peninsula
    Any day (do a section, or all 26 km in long summer daylight)Dromana to Cape Schanck (about 60 km from Melbourne)Free

    Blue-wren-signed trail from Arthurs Seat's steep gravel climbs through Greens Bush to the Cape Schanck cliffs; carry plenty of water and start early on warm days.

  • Darby River to Tongue Point, Wilsons Promontory NP
    Any dayDarby River carpark, Wilsons Prom (about 3 hrs from Melbourne)Free

    A coastal walk over an ancient dune and granite-studded heath to Tongue Point, with a detour to sheltered Fairy Cove for a swim; wallabies are common. A summer day trip or weekend away.

Bike trails

  • Arthurs Seat State Park mountain bike trails
    Any daySeawinds, Upper or Hillview (Dromana) carparks, Arthurs SeatFree

    Over 25 km of singletrack and management tracks on steep rocky terrain, for all abilities; Hillview has water and a bike repair station, the others need your own water.

  • Forrest Mountain Bike Trails, Great Otway NP
    Any day (built for year-round riding)Forrest, Otways (about 2.5 hrs from Melbourne)Free

    About 70 km of singletrack and new flow trails through tall eucalypts, native bush and fern gullies, from a small-town base; the Otways' cool forest suits early summer.

  • Port Fairy Rail Trail
    Any dayWarrnambool to Port Fairy (about 260 km SW of Melbourne)Free

    38 km of asphalt and fine gravel past Koroit and Tower Hill to Port Fairy, about two hours' brisk riding; pair it with a Great Ocean Road summer weekend.

  • East Gippsland Rail Trail
    Any dayBairnsdale to Orbost (about 280 km east of Melbourne)Free

    96 km through farmland and forest with old trestle bridges and Gippsland Lakes glimpses, ending at the long Snowy River trestle; good as a multi-day summer tour.

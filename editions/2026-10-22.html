Hikes

  • Panorama Circuit, The Pines Flora and Fauna Reserve
    Any day (orchids at their best in spring)Frankston NorthFree

    6.2 km loop through coastal heath to a lookout over Melbourne, the Dandenongs and the bay; spring brings out Rabbit Ears, Greenhood and Spider orchids.

  • Dandenong Ranges Botanic Garden circuit
    Daily, last entry 4:30pmOlindaFree

    Easy 3.5 km paved loop at peak rhododendron and azalea season; go early on weekends as parking fills, and note the nearby Olinda VW Track area of the national park is closed for planned-burn prep.

  • Masons Falls and Mt Sugarloaf, Kinglake National Park
    Any dayKinglake National ParkFree

    Short walk to a waterfall that runs well after winter rain, plus the Mt Sugarloaf summit for city views; the Masons Falls BBQs are closed, so bring a picnic.

  • Point Nepean National Park
    Daily, vehicles 6am to 8pm in daylight savingPortsea, Mornington PeninsulaFree

    Walk or ride the shared track to the Quarantine Station and Fort Nepean for big coastal views; part of the Engine House track is closed after a landslip.

Bike trails

  • Lysterfield Park mountain bike trails
    Any dayLysterfieldFree

    24 km of trails including the 2006 Commonwealth Games course, with easy green loops too; spring is when the winter mud dries out.

  • Red Hill Rail Trail
    Any dayMerricks to Red Hill, Mornington PeninsulaFree

    Short 6.3 km gravel trail; climb up to Red Hill for lunch and enjoy the downhill run back.

  • Great Victorian Rail Trail, Mansfield to Merton
    Any day (check closures first)MansfieldFree

    76 km return ride across Lake Eildon with Mt Buller on the horizon; some other sections remain closed after the bushfires, so check the trail's updates before you go.
